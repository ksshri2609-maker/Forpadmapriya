PADMAPRIYA LOVE WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: website content
- style.css: styling (works on phones and computers, light and dark mode)
- script.js: menu, memories add/delete, 100 reasons, song picker and final question
- assets/: the photos shown in More Memories Together

Open on a computer: double-click index.html.

Put it online so it opens on phones (free):
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop
3. Drag the extracted folder (the one containing index.html) into the page.
4. Netlify gives you a link you can send by WhatsApp.

Memories page:
- Add: tap "Choose photos", pick one or more, add a caption, tap "Add to memories".
- Delete: tap the x on a photo, then Delete. "Bring back removed photos" restores deleted ones.
- Added and deleted photos are saved in the browser of the phone or computer being used.
  They do not appear on other devices. To make a photo permanent for everyone, copy it into
  the assets folder and add a line for it in the DEFAULTS list in script.js.

Internet is needed for the optional Google Fonts; fallback fonts are included.
